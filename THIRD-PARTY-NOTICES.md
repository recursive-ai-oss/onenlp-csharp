## Distilled encoder (`student-v1`)

The distilled multilingual encoder (`recipes/accurate/student-v1/PROVENANCE.md`) was trained to reproduce the hidden states of **mmBERT-base** (Marone et al. 2025; MIT licence, JHU CLSP) on text from **FineWeb-2** and **FineWeb** (Hugging Face; ODC-By 1.0, subject to the Common Crawl terms of use) and **Wikipedia** (the Wikipedia contributors; CC BY-SA 3.0 and GFDL). Its token table is initialised from mmBERT's, and it uses mmBERT's tokenizer. The training data includes share-alike text.

## Test fixtures

`tests/OneNlp.Tests/Matching/expected.json.gz` records the output of spaCy 3.8.18 (MIT licence, Copyright Explosion) on documents and patterns written for this repository. It is used only by tests.

`tests/OneNlp.Tests/NounChunks/cases.json.gz` records the noun chunks spaCy 3.8.18 (MIT licence, Copyright Explosion) returns on randomly generated trees and on the tree shapes (heads, relations and parts of speech, with placeholder words) of the Universal Dependencies English-EWT development set (CC BY-SA 4.0, credit: Universal Dependencies English Web Treebank). It is used only by tests.

`testdata/accurate/` holds randomly initialised tiny encoders (a two-layer BERT-style one and a four-layer ModernBERT-style one, width 16) exported to ONNX, a hand-written WordPiece `tokenizer.json` and PyTorch hidden states for fixed ids, all generated by `tools/accurate/make_tiny_fixture.py` and `make_tiny_modernbert_fixture.py`; `tiny-student.*` is a random two-layer encoder with a factorised embedding from `tools/distill/make_tiny_student_fixture.py`. No released model or third-party text is in it.
